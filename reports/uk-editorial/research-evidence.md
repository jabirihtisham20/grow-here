## Cannibalisation decisions

The original 22-file inventory was compared by title, primary keyword, headings and full text. The final batch has one primary intent per draft. Existing broad pages remain the overview destinations. No legitimate existing text, author, date or URL was edited.

The following attractive candidates were not developed because their proposed angle was too close to current content or required evidence unavailable here: generic home battery economics, solar-export-versus-storage basics, thermostat setbacks, generic standby-power tips, another digital detox and a separate condensation-behind-wardrobes page. They remain candidates or expansion opportunities, not additional articles in the batch.

Closely related new pages have deliberate boundaries: laundry is room/layout suitability; dehumidifier cost is arithmetic; plug-in monitoring is measurement technique. Condensation is symptom recording and referral, not laundry optimisation. Fridge rotation is daily prioritisation; freezer planning is inventory and outage preparation. These boundaries need confirmation against database-only drafts and real Search Console queries once access is restored.
